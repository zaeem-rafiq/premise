# Early model measurement, 2026-10-04

The question (plan 2.2a): on the real tide pool seed, can Workers AI models complete the seeded
requests, how long does a change take, do energy creatures name the shared quantity, and do the models
adapt when `ENERGY_MAX` changes under them?

Method: `npm run measure:models -- --early --model <id>` (`05-agent.md` section 6, early variant). The
product's own model loop runs against an in-memory copy of `seeds/tidepool/` and calls Workers AI over
REST. There is no Trunk, no premise engine and no push in it. Per model: the 12 additive requests A01 to
A12, one run each, and the two hero requests P1a and P1b, five runs each, each with one scripted heads-up
after `ENERGY_MAX` changes from 100 to 1. Result files and transcripts are beside this file.

## What the runs showed

- The models write the crab's threshold as a share of `ENERGY_MAX` (`ENERGY_MAX * 0.3`) even when the
  request says "below 30". That code is correct under both scales, so a crab written by these models does
  not break when the scale changes. The shrimp ("spends 5 energy per dart") is written with the absolute
  number and does break. The side-by-side comparison with the plain merge must therefore rest on the
  shrimp or on the canned cast of `02-tidepool.md` section 7, not on a crab written live.
- After the heads-up, `gpt-oss-120b` changed `drain(shrimp, 5)` to `0.05` or to `ENERGY_MAX * 0.05` and
  said so in one sentence. Where it had written a share, it answered that no change was needed.
- A08 was saved under another file name by all three models, A12 by two. That points at the request
  wording or at the expected name, not at one model.
- Every hero run of `glm-5.3-flash` ended with `model_error:model_rejected`, while its additive runs
  completed. The runner does not record the status and body of a rejection, so the cause is not known
  yet. The request after a heads-up is the likely place to look.
- 22 runs at once drew no rate refusal on any model.

## Limits of this evidence

- One run per additive request, and five per hero request. The shares are first readings.
- The script scores by parsing the final files. It runs no code a model wrote.
- The first attempt of this measurement completed 0 of 22 runs: the request sent the tool definitions as
  text and the service refused it (error 5006). That was a defect in `buildModelRequest`, fixed in commit
  `e51ab17`. The numbers above are from runs after the fix.

## Decision

- Workhorse: `@cf/openai/gpt-oss-120b`. It is the fastest and cheapest of the three and adapted best.
- Race: `@cf/moonshotai/kimi-k2.7-code` is usable. `@cf/zai-org/glm-5.3-flash` is usable for additive
  requests and stays out of any run with a heads-up until its rejection is understood.
- Slice 12 (Race) stays in the plan.
- Follow-ups: record the status and a redacted body for every `model_rejected`; find why `glm-5.3-flash`
  refuses the request after a heads-up; check the expected file names of A08 and A12; read the unclear
  P1b runs. The measurement is repeated on the engine at M3 (plan 2.5).
